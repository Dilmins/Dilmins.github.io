About

Driving innovation through engineering

As a Mechatronics Engineering graduate of General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University, I specialise in building intelligent systems that bridge hardware and software, from EEG-driven exoskeletons to real-world IoT deployments serving thousands of commuters across Sri Lanka.

My current role as a Mechatronic Engineer at C3 Labs has taken me from PCB design and embedded firmware to deploying large-format E-Paper transit displays at major public bus stands in Colombo, a live production system I built from scratch.

Beyond engineering, I took part in the AOTS Leadership for Business Growth program in Japan (JASTECA 2026), gaining exposure to ESG, Japanese management philosophy and sustainable innovation. I believe great engineers understand both the technical and the human side of what they build.
